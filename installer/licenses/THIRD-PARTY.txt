Downloads use the HTTPS support that Windows itself provides (WinInet), so no OpenSSL or other cryptography library is
installed with the program.

If a sqlite3.dll was put in the installer's redist folder, it is carried too: SQLite is in the public domain
(https://www.sqlite.org/copyright.html).  Without it the MySword and theWord readers use the winsqlite3.dll that
Windows 10 and 11 include.
